INVESTMENT BUSINESS - iPHONE / iPAD
===================================

Apple does not allow installing app files directly on an iPhone.
Use the web link instead. It installs like an app and works offline.

Install (takes 30 seconds)
  1. Open the game's web link in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow).
  3. Tap "Add to Home Screen", then "Add".
  4. Open "Investment Business" from the home screen.

Files in this folder (for the App Store only, not for players)
  Investment-Business-ios-xcode-project.zip
      Open on a Mac in Xcode, choose your Apple Developer team under
      Signing & Capabilities, then Product > Archive > Distribute App.
      Needs an Apple Developer account.
  Investment-Business-ios-unsigned.ipa
      Unsigned build. It cannot be installed on a normal iPhone.
