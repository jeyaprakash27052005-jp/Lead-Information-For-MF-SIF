Privacy Policy

Mcube Academy FinFun by Mcube Academy · Last updated 17 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Mcube Academy games collect, why, where it is kept and how to have it deleted. It applies to the games website and to every Mcube Academy game app on Android, iPhone, iPad, Windows and Mac: Investment Business, Money Adventure, ₹1 Lakh → ₹10 Lakh Challenge, Bull vs Bear – Aadu Puli, Pallanguli Investment Game, Personal Finance Management and Unlock Your Future.

In short: you register with your name and mobile number to play. We save that and your game result. We use it to run the game and, because you agree, to contact you about Mcube Academy courses and investor education. We do not show ads, we do not sell your information, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

1. Information we collect

Only what a player types in the registration form, the game result, and the type of device. Nothing is collected before you register.

The games do not collect your location, contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera, advertising ID, payment details or browsing history. They contain no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking.

2. How we use it

  • To run the game: register you, keep each mobile number to one play per game, and show and keep your result.
  • To contact you about Mcube Academy courses, events and investor education, only because you ticked the agreement box when you registered. You can ask us to stop at any time.
  • To see how many people play each game.
  • Bull vs Bear – Aadu Puli and Unlock Your Future show a daily Top 10 leaderboard to everyone who plays: only the player's first name (and team-mate's first name) and the score, time or result amount. Mobile numbers, email and age are never shown.

Games use play money only. Nothing in them is financial advice.

3. Where it is kept and who can see it

Your information is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and stored in Google Cloud Firestore, a database service that Mcube Academy uses. Only authorised Mcube Academy staff use it. If you play without internet, it is kept on your device until it can be sent. Investment Business also keeps a copy of the players' names and numbers on the device it is played on, for staff at events.

We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not share it with anyone else except Google Cloud, which stores it for us, or where the law requires us to.

4. How long we keep it

We keep player information while it is needed for the purposes above, and we delete it when you ask us to (see below).

5. Children

Money Adventure is made for children. Before anything is typed in, a grown-ups check asks a simple question, and then a parent or guardian registers with the parent's name and mobile number and agrees to this policy. The game does not ask for the child's name or any other information about the child. Investment Business has a "Kids" mode that is played with a parent, who registers with the parent's mobile number and email. Parents can ask us to delete this information at any time.

6. Delete your information

To have your registration and results deleted, contact us using the details below and tell us the mobile number you registered with and, if you know it, the game you played. We delete the information for that number from our database within 30 days and let you know when it is done. You do not need an account to ask.

To remove the copy kept on your own device, uninstall the app or clear the website data in your browser.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we update this page and the date at the top.
